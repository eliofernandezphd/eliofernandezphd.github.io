Urban–Rural Planning

How city growth and policy reach rural Florida.

Rural places often absorb the costs of urban growth. I study how state and local policy plays out across the urban–rural divide.

A Short History of Urban–Rural Planning

Planners have argued for more than a century about how cities and the countryside depend on each other. The divide has always been blurrier than the maps suggest.

Show the timelineHide the timeline 13 moments, 1898–2024
  1. Early ideas
  2. 1898

    The town-country magnet

    Ebenezer Howard's garden city idea tries to combine the best of city and countryside in one planned community.

  3. 1915

    The city-region

    Patrick Geddes's Cities in Evolution treats a city and its surrounding region as one connected system.

  4. 1923

    Regional planning in the U.S.

    Lewis Mumford, Benton MacKaye, and others form the Regional Planning Association of America to plan whole regions, not just cities.

  5. 1933

    Tennessee Valley Authority

    The federal government takes on large-scale regional planning in the rural South, from dams to electricity to land management.

  6. Growth at the edge
  7. 1956

    Interstate highways

    The Federal-Aid Highway Act speeds suburban and exurban growth into farmland at the urban fringe.

  8. 1981

    Farmland Protection Policy Act

    Federal projects must consider how they convert farmland to other uses.

  9. Rethinking the divide
  10. 1998

    Rural-urban interactions

    Cecilia Tacoli argues flows of people, goods, money, and waste between city and country matter more than the line between them.

  11. 2011

    Rural America in an urban society

    Daniel Lichter and David Brown show how rural places are tied into urban economies, labor markets, and politics.

  12. 2014

    Planetary urbanization

    Neil Brenner and Christian Schmid argue urbanization reshapes even remote landscapes through mines, farms, and infrastructure.

  13. Rural environmental justice
  14. 2016

    Urban-rural exploitation

    Kelly-Reif and Wing name the pattern of cities exporting burdens like waste, pollution, and industrial farming to rural communities.

  15. 2016

    Rural voices in EJ

    David Pellow calls on environmental justice and rural studies scholars to work together, and Ashwood and MacTavish show how rural residents get outvoted on decisions that put burdens in their communities.

  16. 2019

    Urban-Rural Linkages

    UN-Habitat publishes guiding principles for planning cities and rural areas together.

  17. 2024

    Landscapes in transition

    Brown and colleagues show how climate-driven landscape changes in Europe, from energy projects to conservation, create new rural justice conflicts.

In Florida

Florida’s cities depend on rural land for food, water, and space for what cities produce, from farmland to waste. Rural counties often have fewer planning resources to shape how that relationship works.

  1. 1979

    Right to Farm Act

    Florida protects farm operations from nuisance claims. Later amendments bar local governments from restricting farm activities covered by state best management practices.

  2. 1985

    Growth Management Act

    Every city and county must adopt a comprehensive plan, including how rural land is used.

  3. 1999

    Florida Forever

    The state launches a land conservation program that buys and protects natural and rural lands.

  4. 2011

    Community Planning Act

    State review of local plans is scaled back, leaving rural counties with fewer planning resources to manage growth pressure.

  5. 2026

    Biosolids flows

    My research traces how biosolids produced in Florida's metro counties are applied in less populated rural counties.
